⛸️ Beginner's Guide to Ice Skating

⛸️

📋 Basic Rules

  • Bend Your Knees
  • Keep Your Eyes Up and Follow Traffic
  • Keep Arms Out in Front
  • Protect Your Hands When Falling

⛸️ Ice Skating Equipment

  • Ice Skates
  • Skate Guards
  • Blade Soakers
  • Helmet or Protective Pads

💃 Basic Techniques

  • Balanced Stance & Gliding
  • Forward Swizzles (Hourglass)
  • Forward Stroking
  • Snowplow Stop
